01

Your Mission

Solve one workplace problem well.

Your team will design and build an AI agent for Contoso Thailand employees. You may focus on Workplace, Technology, Business Travel, or combine them where it creates a better user experience.

✦

There is no single correct solution. A focused agent that works reliably is better than a large agent with many unfinished features.

Choose a real problem Be specific about the user and the outcome.
Use the provided resources Pick only the knowledge and data relevant to your idea.
Show something unique Go beyond the baseline with your own design choices.

06 · Judging

Build something useful — and prove it works.

25% Business usefulness
20% Knowledge use & accuracy
20% Tool implementation
15% Agent instructions & behaviour
15% Creativity / innovation
5% Demo quality

Before time is up

Your showcase should make these five things clear.

  1. What workplace problem does your agent solve?
  2. How does it use the provided knowledge?
  3. What important behaviour did you configure in the instructions?
  4. What does your tool do?
  5. What makes your solution different or especially useful?
